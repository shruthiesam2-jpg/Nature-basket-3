Why Nature Basket

The problem

Produce passes through many hands. Farmers get a small share, shoppers pay more, and food spoils on the way.

Our solution

One short chain: Farmer → Nature Basket → Customer. We aggregate, check quality and deliver within a day or two of harvest.

Our mission

Raise small farmers' income, cut grocery bills and make every meal traceable to the field.
